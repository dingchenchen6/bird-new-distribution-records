# Task Summary / 结果摘要

## English

The strict Chinese bird species pool contained 1505 species across 26 orders.
Direct matching placed 1456 checklist species and 500 corrected newly recorded species onto the McTavish tree.
The explicit taxonomy bridge recovered an additional 16 checklist species and 19 newly recorded species.
After direct matching and transparent bridging, the final pruned Chinese bird phylogeny contained 1472 tree tips.
Corrected newly recorded species were documented in 23 orders; the richest order was Passeriformes with 289 newly recorded species, representing 33.6% of the Chinese species pool in that order.
Unresolved species were retained in the audit tables for transparent review, rather than being silently dropped or force-matched.

## 中文

严格筛选后的中国鸟类种级物种库共包含 1505 个物种、26 个目。
直接匹配可将 1456 个中国名录物种和 500 个校正后新纪录物种挂接到 McTavish 系统树上。
通过透明的 taxonomy bridge，又额外恢复了 16 个中国名录物种和 19 个新纪录物种的树上位置。
经过直接匹配与名称桥接后，最终用于绘图的中国鸟类系统树子树包含 1472 个 tip。
校正后的新纪录物种分布于 23 个目中，其中新纪录物种数最多的目是 Passeriformes，共 289 个物种，占中国该目物种库的 33.6%。
所有未解决名称均保留在审计表中供人工复核，而不是被静默删除或不透明地强制匹配。
